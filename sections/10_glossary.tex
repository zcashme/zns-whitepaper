\section{Glossary}
\label{sec:glossary}

Terms defined in the body are collected here for reference. Their normative definitions remain in the referenced sections.

\small
\begin{longtable}{@{}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.26\textwidth}p{0.70\textwidth}@{}}
\toprule
Term & Definition \\
\midrule
\endhead
\bottomrule
\endfoot
\zns{} (Zcash Name Service) & The name registry protocol specified by this document. \\
Name Note & An Ironwood output whose memo records a name transition, including its \field{expires\_at} value, and whose \zns{}-specific note-commitment inputs are derived from that transition; see Section~\ref{sec:name-notes}. \\
Request & A user-to-Mint memo asking the Mint to evaluate a \action{claim}, \action{update}, or \action{release}, with no OTP field; see Section~\ref{sec:requests}. \\
Relay & A Mint-to-user memo of the form \texttt{ZNS:otp:<otp>:<name>:<verb>:<ua>} that delivers the OTP; see Section~\ref{sec:authorization}. \\
Respond & A user-to-Mint memo that returns the OTP, \texttt{ZNS:update:<name>:<ua>:<otp>} or \texttt{ZNS:release:<name>:<ua>:<otp>}; see Section~\ref{sec:requests} and Section~\ref{sec:authorization}. \\
Transition & A \action{claim}, \action{update}, or \action{release} recorded in a Name Note and represented by the tuple $\sigma$; see Section~\ref{sec:name-notes}. \\
Predecessor (\field{prev\_rcm}) & The \field{rcm} of the previously accepted Name Note for the same name, encoded as 64 lowercase hexadecimal characters. The initial claim uses 64 zeroes; see Section~\ref{sec:name-notes}. \\
Binding & The association between a name and a Unified Address established by its accepted Name Note history. \\
Registry state & The set of name bindings derived from accepted Name Notes in canonical chain order; see Section~\ref{sec:resolver}. \\
Name Note viewing key & The published full viewing key for the Mint account used to create Name Notes. \\
Mint & The protocol's single registrar. It evaluates name requests under the naming policy and creates Name Notes for accepted requests; see Section~\ref{sec:the-mint}. \\
Mint authorization & Authorization provided by the recognized Mint spending key and accepted subject to the attestation requirements in Section~\ref{sec:the-mint}. \\
Resolver & A client that scans the canonical Zcash chain using the Name Note viewing key, verifies Name Notes, derives registry state according to Section~\ref{sec:resolver}, and provides current and historical binding state, including committed expiration values. \\
User authorization & Request, Relay, and Respond, used to authorize an \action{update} or controller-requested \action{release}. For an \action{update}, the OTP is bound to \texttt{(name, action, ua, expires\_at)}; see Section~\ref{sec:authorization}. \\
One-time passcode (OTP) & A six-digit decimal passcode delivered in the Relay and returned in the Respond; see Section~\ref{sec:authorization}. \\
Expiration (\field{expires\_at}) & The absolute Unix timestamp in whole seconds committed by a \action{claim} or \action{update} Name Note, or the exact value \texttt{none} for a registration without fixed expiration. \\
Registration term & A duration requested for an initial fixed-term registration or renewal. The user supplies a term; the Mint computes the resulting absolute \field{expires\_at}; see Section~\ref{sec:the-mint-lifecycle}. \\
Median Time Past (MTP) & The chain-derived time value obtained from the canonical Zcash chain and used by the Mint for protocol-defined time checks. \\
Liveness time & The canonical-chain MTP associated with the block containing the accepted \action{claim} or most recent accepted \action{update} for a registration. \\
Liveness deadline & The current liveness time plus the protocol-defined liveness interval. Reaching the deadline requires the Mint to create a \action{release} Name Note. \\
ZNSIP & A \zns{} Improvement Proposal specifying a proposed protocol change; see Section~\ref{sec:znsips}. \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

\noindent\textsuperscript{*}External terms inherited from the Zcash protocol specification \cite{zcash-protocol} or its ZIPs are not redefined here: Ironwood action, Ironwood memo, note commitment (\field{cmx}), Unified Address, and incoming viewing key retain the meaning fixed by their normative sources \cite{zip224,zip316,zcash-protocol}.
